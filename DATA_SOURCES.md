# Data sources

| Source | Used for | License | Checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Wyscout soccer match event dataset](https://figshare.com/collections/Soccer_match_event_dataset/4415000) (Pappalardo et al., 2019): players, teams, matches, events of the 2017/18 season in England, Spain, Germany, Italy, France | Real players, minutes, per-90 stats, similarity vectors | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) | 2026-10-03 |
| Synthetic (generated by `tools/seed`) | 1,397 "Demo Player" rows to reach 5,000 for benchmarks, all injuries, non-fit availability | Generated, marked `is_synthetic` | n/a |

Attribution: Pappalardo, L., Cintia, P., Rossi, A. et al. *A public data set of spatio-temporal match events in soccer competitions.* Scientific Data 6, 236 (2019). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-019-0247-7

Notes:

- Stats are from the 2017/18 season; "current team" means the team a player played most minutes for that season.
- Medical data is entirely synthetic and only ever attached to synthetic players.
- Raw files are downloaded to `data/raw/` (not in git) and pinned by SHA-256 in `tools/seed/src/index.ts`.
